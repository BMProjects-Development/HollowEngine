<center><h1>Sounds</h1></center>

*Play sounds to the world and individual players.*

![Sounds](/docs/title-sounds.webp)

---

## `playSound(sound, position, volume, pitch)` – sound in the world

Plays the sound at the specified world position for all nearby players. Parameters: `sound` (required), `position` (required), `volume` (default `1.0`), `pitch` (default `1.0`).

```kotlin
playSound(
    sound = "minecraft:entity.creeper.primed",
    position = pos(10.0, 64.0, 10.0),
    volume = 1.0,
    pitch = 1.0
)

// With default settings
playSound("minecraft:entity.creeper.primed", pos(10.0, 64.0, 10.0))
```

---

## `playSound(player, sound, volume, pitch)` – Sound to the player

Overload for playing sound to a specific player:

```kotlin
playSound(player = player, sound = "minecraft:entity.experience_orb.pickup", volume = 1.0, pitch = 1.0)
```

---

## `player.playSound(sound, volume, pitch)` – alternate call

Method on `Player` (see [Player](/docs/hollowengine/scripting/player)):

```kotlin
player.playSound("minecraft:entity.experience_orb.pickup", 1.0, 1.0)
```

---

## Example: Zone with sound

```kotlin
val pos = pos(100.0, 64.0, 100.0)
say("Go to the marked area!")

val who = player.waitZone(position = pos, radius = 3.0, leave = false)
playSound(
    sound = "minecraft:block.note_block.chime",
    position = pos,
    volume = 1.0,
    pitch = 1.0
)
say("You entered the zone!")
```
