<center><h1>Navigation</h1></center>

*How an NPC finds its way and walks it, and how to tune that for one NPC or one move.*

Every engine NPC finds and walks its paths the same way, whatever moves it: a script's [`move`](/docs/hollowengine/scripting/npc/movement), a goal such as attacking, or the patrol component. The **Navigation** component tunes this for one NPC. Without it the NPC goes by the defaults shown below, and a script can change them for a single move.

---

## The Navigation component

Open the NPC's components with the NPC wand and add **Navigation** (`hollowengine:entity/navigation`). Its settings come in four groups. The component lives on the server only: clients see the NPC move and need nothing of it.

### Path

| Field | Default | Meaning |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Straighten the path | on | Walks the found path in straight lines wherever nothing is in the way, instead of block by block. A straight line never cuts across doors, hazards, jumps or anything the NPC avoids. |
| Drop off ledges | on | Steps off ledges higher than a block, as high as the fall damage below allows, instead of looking for a way down. Water at the bottom takes a fall from any height. |
| Fall damage | `0` | The fall damage, in half-hearts, the NPC accepts for a shorter way. `0` falls only as far as is safe: 3 blocks for a regular NPC. |

### Jumps

| Field | Default | Meaning |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Over gaps | on | Jumps over gaps onto ground as high as the ground it takes off from. |
| Over gaps, up | on | Jumps over gaps onto ground a block higher. |
| Over gaps, down | on | Jumps over gaps onto ground one or two blocks lower. |
| Widest gap | `2` | In blocks. |
| Jump cost | `3` | How many blocks of walking a jump is worth: the NPC jumps when going around is longer by more than this. It also counts for jumping up a block. |
| Run-up | `1.5` | How much faster than its own speed the NPC runs up to a jump over a gap. |

### Movement

| Field | Default | Meaning |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Acceleration | `0.3` | Seconds from standing to full speed. |
| Deceleration | `0.5` | Seconds from full speed to a stop. The NPC brakes this early for the end of its path, for sharp turns and before a jump. |
| Speed on straights | `1.1` | How much faster the NPC walks on a long straight stretch. `1` never speeds up. |
| Straight length | `8` | In blocks: how long the straight stretch ahead has to be. The extra speed fades out over the last half of it. |
| Step back | `2` | In blocks: the longest walk to a point behind the NPC it takes backward instead of turning round. `0` always turns. It does not step back up or down a block. |
| Backward speed | `0.6` | The NPC's speed walking backward, as a share of walking forward. |
| Sideways speed | `0.8` | The NPC's speed walking sideways, as a share of walking forward. |

### Avoiding

| Field | Default | Meaning |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Swim | on | Swims through deep water. Without it, water deeper than a block is a wall to the NPC; one that stands in it already gets out to the nearest shore. |
| Water cost | `8` | How many blocks of walking a block of water is worth, so a swimming NPC still takes a bridge. |
| Blocks | empty | Blocks the NPC walks around, each with a cost. |

An entry of **Blocks** is a block id such as `minecraft:farmland`, or a block tag such as `#minecraft:flowers`, and a cost: how many blocks of walking stepping onto or into such a block is worth. A negative cost means never. Both the floor the NPC stands on and the blocks its body is in count, so farmland, carpets and crops all work; a block that matches several entries costs the most of them.

An NPC that already stands on a block it may never step on, or in water it does not swim in, is not stuck there: it walks out of it the shortest way and does not come back.

## Changing the settings for one move

`MoveOptions(navigation = ...)` changes the settings for that move only. It is given the NPC's own settings, or the defaults when it has no component, and returns the ones to use:

```kotlin
npc.move(
    pos(40, 64, 12),
    MoveOptions(navigation = { copy(jumps = jumps.copy(maxGap = 3, runUp = 2f)) }),
)
```

Zones to keep out of are set from scripts as well, for one move or for good: see [Keeping out of zones](/docs/hollowengine/scripting/npc/movement#keeping-out-of-zones).

## Colliders and blocking bodies

All mobs, not only NPCs, plan their paths around [solid colliders](/docs/hollowengine/scripting/colliders) and around the boxes of blocking bodies, the way they plan around blocks: a mob no longer walks into a 3×3 machine standing on what looks like empty floor. When such a collider moves into or out of the way of a mob walking a path, the mob plans the path again, as after a block change.

The top of a collider is not yet ground a path goes over, so a low solid collider, such as a rug, is walked around rather than stepped onto.

## Animations

The standard player model plays its walk and run clips as fast as keeps its feet planted on the ground, walking backward plays the walk backward, and a sideways step plays the walk. For a model of your own, drive the clip speed in its [animator](/docs/hollowengine/scripting/animator-editor) the same way: `movement_animation_speed` is the entity's speed in blocks a second, negative when it walks backward, so divide it by how fast a planted foot of your clip moves over the ground, and use `local_side_speed`, its sideways speed in blocks a tick, for a clip that steps sideways.
