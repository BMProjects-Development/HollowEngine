# Decals

A decal projects an image onto surfaces: footprints, puddles, markings, graffiti or a projector image.

## Placement

- Drag a `.png` from assets onto the IDE's game view to create a [world object](/docs/hollowengine/world/objects) with a decal.
- In a [rig](/docs/hollowengine/models/rig-editor), add a **Decal** attachment to a bone or the whole model. Its projection follows the attachment.
- In the VFX editor, add a **Decal** node. A standalone node creates one projection; a direct child of an emitter creates one for each particle, using that particle's size, rotation and color.

Choose a texture such as `project:textures/decals/mark.png`. With the [Media addon](/docs/hollowengine/addons/media), you can also use `.mp4`, `.webm`, `.mkv`, `.mov` or `.avi` video.

## Projection

A decal projects along its own down axis. Rotate it to point at a wall or another surface.

| Mode | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **BOX** | Parallel projection through a box volume: puddles, footprints and stickers. |
| **PROJECTOR** | Projection from a point, widening with distance: slides and video projectors. |

**Width** and **Length** control image size and proportions. Leaving length unset preserves the source image's proportions. **Reach** limits projection depth; when unset, the decal looks for a surface below. A projector's **Field of view** controls its opening angle.

**Color** tints the image, **Opacity** controls visibility, and **Angle fade** removes it from surfaces at steep angles. Normal, additive and multiply blending are available. **Emissive** makes the image independent of world lighting.

In VFX, opacity and tint can use expressions and timeline keys. They multiply the decal's own opacity and color, for example to fade out a footprint.
